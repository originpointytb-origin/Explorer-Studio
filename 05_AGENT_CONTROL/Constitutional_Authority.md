Constitutional Governance
Constitutional Authority
The Explorer Universe Constitution is the highest governing document of the Explorer Universe.
Its principles take precedence over every subordinate document, guideline, production system, or creative work.

Constitutional Interpretation
The Constitution shall be interpreted according to its enduring principles rather than isolated wording.
Whenever uncertainty arises, the interpretation that best preserves the First Truth, stewardship, integrity, humility,
wonder, faithful remembrance, and responsibility shall prevail.
Constitutional Consistency
No official document, production, story, character, institution, or future world shall knowingly contradict the
Constitution.
Where a conflict is discovered, the Constitution shall remain the governing authority until formally amended.
Constitutional Amendments
The Constitution is intended to remain stable across generations.
Amendments shall therefore be exceptional rather than routine.
A constitutional amendment should occur only when:
production experience demonstrates a genuine constitutional need,
- 
a contradiction requiring resolution is discovered,
- 
a principle requires clarification to preserve long-term consistency, or
- 
the Explorer Universe reaches a stage of development that cannot be faithfully governed without constitutional
expansion.
- 
Amendments shall never be made merely to follow creative preference, temporary trends, or stylistic revision.
Production Relationship
The Constitution governs production.
Production does not govern the Constitution.
However, production experience may reveal opportunities to strengthen the Constitution through carefully considered


amendments that preserve its identity.
Constitutional Stewardship
Every creator entrusted with the Explorer Universe accepts the responsibility to preserve the integrity of this
Constitution.
The highest duty of every steward is not innovation alone.
It is faithful continuity.
The Constitution shall therefore remain a living foundation—stable in its principles, thoughtful in its growth, and
enduring in its purpose.
Thus is established the Explorer Universe Constitution as the supreme constitutional authority of the Explorer
Universe for the Initial Production Era.
